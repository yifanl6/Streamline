Quick glossary

What is a bounded program?

A spending policy that limits the total amount, expiration, approved recipients, and approved service types before a payment request can be signed.

What does private review verify?

It checks the submitted work against your criteria through an attested NEAR AI environment and returns a decision, reasons, hashes, and verification checks.

Does Streamline replace Sputnik DAO?

No. Sputnik remains the treasury and approval layer. Streamline organizes what the community does with that treasury.
